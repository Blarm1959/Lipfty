How to play

Lipfty

Lipfty is played on a 4×4 board with six black and six white pieces. The pieces belong to both players.

On your turn, either place one unused piece, move any legal board piece one adjacent square, or jump over an adjacent piece into the empty square immediately beyond it.

Movement and jumping can be horizontal, vertical or diagonal. Jumps do not capture. After a jump you may continue jumping with the same piece, changing direction if you wish.

You may not move the piece your opponent moved on their immediately preceding turn. A newly placed piece is not protected.

Win by being the player who creates four pieces of the same colour in a complete row, column or main diagonal.
