How to play

Lipfty

Lipfty is played on a 4×4 board with six black and six white pieces. The pieces belong to both players.

While pieces remain off the board, your opponent chooses Black or White for your turn. You must use that colour: either place a new piece, move an existing piece one adjacent square, or jump with an existing piece.

If you move or jump instead of placing, your opponent must place the colour you give them on their next turn. This prevents the placement part of the game from stalling.

Once all 12 pieces are on the board, colour-giving ends. On each turn you may move or jump any piece of either colour.

Movement and jumping can be horizontal, vertical or diagonal. Jumps do not capture. After a jump you may continue jumping with the same piece, changing direction if you wish.

You may not move the piece your opponent moved on their immediately preceding turn. A newly placed piece is not protected.

Win immediately when your action creates four pieces of the same colour in a complete row, column or main diagonal — including during a multi-jump.
