How to play

Lipfty

Objective. Lipfty is played on a 4×4 board with six pieces of each chosen colour. The pieces belong to both players. Win by creating four pieces of the same colour in a complete row, column or main diagonal.

Opening play. While pieces remain off the board, the player who has just completed a turn chooses which colour the opponent must use. The opponent may place a new piece of that colour, move an existing piece of that colour one adjacent square, or jump with an existing piece of that colour.

If you move or jump instead of placing during the opening, your opponent must place the colour you give them on their next turn. This ensures that all 12 pieces eventually reach the board.

Moving and jumping. Moves and jumps may be horizontal, vertical or diagonal. A jump goes over an adjacent piece into the empty square immediately beyond it. After jumping, you may continue jumping with the same piece and may change direction.

During one multi-jump you may not loop back onto a square already visited. If Undo Previous Jump is enabled in Settings, an immediate jump back to the square just left is allowed, but longer loops are still not allowed. If no legal further jump is available, the turn ends automatically.

You may not move the piece your opponent moved on their immediately preceding turn. A newly placed piece is not protected.

End game. Once all 12 pieces are on the board, colour-giving ends and either colour may be moved. The first jump in a turn now removes the piece that was jumped over. Any further jumps in the same turn do not remove another piece.

The opponent must use their next turn to put the removed piece back on any empty square except the square from which it was removed. Replacing the piece uses the whole turn and may itself create a winning position.

After a replacement. The player who caused that removal then gets one normal turn in which they may move or jump, but a jump on that turn does not remove another piece. This prevents repeated jump-remove-replace turns from denying the opponent normal play. After that turn, first-jump removal becomes active again.

End-game limit. The default is 12 end-game moves in total — 6 moves each. Settings can extend this to 18 or 24 total moves. If nobody has won when the selected limit is reached, the game is a draw. A win created on the final allowed move takes precedence over the draw.

Winning options. The standard win is four matching-colour pieces in a complete row, column or main diagonal. Settings can additionally allow a matching 2×2 square and/or matching pieces in all four corners as wins. A win takes effect immediately, including during a multi-jump or forced replacement.

Undo and timer. If last-move Undo is enabled, the previous play can be undone even after a win, including a computer win. The move timer may be set to 30, 45 or 60 seconds, or Unlimited. Reaching zero does not force the turn to end; the timer stops when the game is won or drawn.
